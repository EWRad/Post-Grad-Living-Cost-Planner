Getting to know your utility bill

Heads up! You don't just pay for electricity by the kWh. Your bill is made up of many different charges, like fixed monthly fees, delivery charges, and taxes, and together they make up what you actually pay for home energy. Michigan Saves has a helpful guide on how to read your energy bill that breaks down what each line means.

Once you know what's on a bill, use the Residential Energy Cost Estimator from NLR to estimate your own utility costs, then enter that number in the Utilities box above.
